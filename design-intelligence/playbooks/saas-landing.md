# Playbook: SaaS Landing Page

**UX priorities**: fast comprehension of what the product does and for whom; credibility-building
appropriate to visitor familiarity (`principles/23-saas-product.md`); one clear primary conversion path.
**Visual priorities**: one reserved display-type moment for the core claim; restrained, purposeful color
(`principles/06-color.md`); real product evidence over generic illustration.
**Information architecture**: consider Stripe's audience-segmented proof over a fixed feature/
testimonial/pricing sequence if buyer personas genuinely differ (`research/awwwards/01-stripe.md`).
**Typical patterns**: `patterns/hero.md`, `patterns/pricing.md`, embedded product demo over static
feature cards where feasible (`research/awwwards/02-framer.md`).
**Appropriate motion**: brand-atmosphere motion (soft, rare, with fallback) plus functional entrance
motion; avoid decorative motion with no stated purpose (`motion/motion-anti-patterns.md`).
**Accessibility considerations**: full `principles/12-accessibility.md` baseline; verify contrast on any
gradient/imagery-backed hero text.
**Responsive priorities**: mobile hero headline scale re-tuned (not proportionally shrunk); primary CTA
thumb-reachable; treat mobile nav as a real design decision, not solved-by-hamburger-default.
**Common mistakes**: generic "Powerful. Simple. Fast." headline; three-column icon-feature grid with no
differentiation; every visitor shown the same pitch regardless of persona; pricing hidden behind
unnecessary friction.
**Quality checklist**: One-sentence product claim specific to this company? One primary CTA? Real
evidence (screenshots/demo/named customers), not generic icons? Mobile hero re-tuned, not just shrunk?
Passes `anti-generic-ai/generic-ui-signatures.md`?
