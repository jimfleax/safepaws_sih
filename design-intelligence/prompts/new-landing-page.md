# Prompt Template: New Landing Page

Use when: building a new marketing/landing page for a product.

**Route to**: `/design-first` then `/premium-ui`, using `playbooks/saas-landing.md` (or the closer-
matching playbook: `ecommerce.md`, `luxury-brand.md`, `agency.md`, etc. — pick by actual product type).

**Fill in before starting**:
- Product, one sentence, specific (not "an AI platform" — what does it actually do).
- Primary audience and whether personas are genuinely heterogeneous (affects segmentation decisions
  per `principles/15-conversion.md`).
- The single primary action this page should drive.
- Any existing brand assets/constraints to work within.

**Then execute `/design-first`**: produce the design brief (`templates/design-brief.md`), explicitly
naming the design family (`tokens/themes/`) and citing 1-3 references from `retrieval/reference-
index.json` for principles (not composition) relevant to this product's category.

**Then execute `/premium-ui`** through to the final quality gate.
