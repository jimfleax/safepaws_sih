# Surface: Pricing

Applying `patterns/pricing.md` to Meridian.

## Structure
Two tiers matching the two confirmed personas, not a generic 3-tier good/better/best ladder invented
without a real basis: **Individual** (solo researcher, per-seat) and **Team** (shared workspaces,
per-seat with a minimum, plus admin/sharing features). No arbitrarily-inflated third "Enterprise" tier
unless a real enterprise motion exists — per `patterns/pricing.md`'s instruction not to force a
three-tier structure onto a two-persona product.

## What differs between tiers (led with differences, not repeated feature lists)
Shared workspace + team member visibility into each other's synthesis drafts is the single stated
differentiator for Team — not a long repeated checklist where most rows are identical checkmarks.

## No dark patterns (per `principles/15-conversion.md`)
No countdown timer, no pre-checked add-ons, no "most popular" badge without real basis — if usage data
later shows a real popular tier, that can be added truthfully then, not invented now for a launch.

## Mobile
Tiers stack with Individual first (the lower-commitment, more common entry point) rather than
preserving an arbitrary desktop left-to-right order with no reasoning behind it.
