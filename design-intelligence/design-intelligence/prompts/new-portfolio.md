# Prompt Template: New Portfolio

Use when: building a personal or studio portfolio site.

**Route to**: `/design-first` then `/premium-ui`, using `playbooks/portfolio.md` (individual) or
`playbooks/agency.md` (studio).

**Fill in before starting**:
- What is this person's/studio's actual specialty, specifically (not "creative professional" — what
  makes their work distinct)?
- Is the audience evaluation-driven (peers, potential clients judging craft) or task-driven (someone
  needing a specific service fast)? This determines how much novelty risk is appropriate
  (`research/synthesis/12-design-tensions.md` tension #2).

**Design direction should answer**: does this site's *structure*, not just its content, demonstrate the
specialty (per Mat Voyce, `research/awwwards/11-mat-voyce.md`)? If the answer is "no, it's a generic
About/Work/Contact template," revise before building.

**Then execute `/premium-ui`**, with `scoring-schema.json`'s `portfolio` context profile for review.
