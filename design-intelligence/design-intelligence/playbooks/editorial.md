# Playbook: Editorial / Publication

Full principle/pattern detail: `principles/21-editorial.md`, `patterns/editorial.md`,
`typography/editorial-typography.md`.
**UX priorities**: sustained reading quality above all else — every motion/interaction decision tested
against "does this help or interrupt reading."
**Visual priorities**: typographic confidence as the primary craft statement (By-Kin,
`research/awwwards/10-by-kin.md`); classical editorial devices used sparingly and consistently.
**Information architecture**: clear separation between content types (articles, sections, issues/
archives) with predictable, convention-following navigation for a returning-reader audience.
**Typical patterns**: narrow reading-width body column, considered pull-quote/caption system,
related-content module placed after (not interrupting) the body.
**Appropriate motion**: "quiet," reading-subordinate transitions only (`research/awwwards/10-by-kin.md`
§8) — motion that draws attention to itself is a direct cost to the core reading experience here.
**Accessibility considerations**: real semantic heading hierarchy within long articles; captions/alt
text for all editorial imagery; no interstitial ads/modals interrupting mid-read.
**Responsive priorities**: line-length and paragraph width re-verified at mobile widths specifically
(`typography/line-length-and-rhythm.md`).
**Common mistakes**: marketing-site conventions (large CTAs, card grids, heavy motion) applied to
content whose job is reading; inconsistent typographic device usage (pull quotes/drop caps applied
arbitrarily).
**Quality checklist**: Line-length within 45-75 characters at all breakpoints? Motion tested against the
"helps or interrupts reading" question? Typographic hierarchy real (semantic), not just visual?
