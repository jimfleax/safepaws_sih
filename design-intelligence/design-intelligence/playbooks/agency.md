# Playbook: Creative Studio / Agency

**UX priorities**: lead with real work, fast — credibility is built by demonstrated craft, not written
pitch (`research/awwwards/04-uncommon-studio.md`).
**Visual priorities**: either a consistent studio-wide craft standard (Obys/Zajno's disciplined
consistency) or an explicit, stated "no fixed style, consistent feeling" philosophy (Resn) — pick one
deliberately, don't default to inconsistency by accident.
**Information architecture**: nav breadth should match actual business model — sparse for boutique/
few-clients studios (Obys's two-item nav), broader if the studio serves many distinct segments.
**Typical patterns**: video-led hero with explicit media controls (`patterns/hero.md`), bespoke
per-project case-study layouts (`patterns/gallery.md`, `patterns/storytelling.md`).
**Appropriate motion**: higher-risk/richer motion is often appropriate here specifically to demonstrate
technical range (`principles/22-creative-web.md`) — but always with real user control over autoplay/
audio (`research/awwwards/04-uncommon-studio.md` §7).
**Accessibility considerations**: never disable pinch-zoom (a real, observed failure even in
award-winning agency work, `research/awwwards/04-uncommon-studio.md` §11); explicit autoplay controls.
**Responsive priorities**: video-heavy case studies need real mobile-specific asset/data-cost
consideration, not just responsive sizing.
**Common mistakes**: an "Awards" claim with nothing to back it up; motion/richness with no connection to
demonstrated capability; a contact/hire-us form that abandons usability convention at the one moment
conversion matters most.
**Quality checklist**: Real work shown immediately? User control over autoplay media? Nav matches
actual client-model breadth? Contact/inquiry flow follows solid form-design convention regardless of how
experimental the rest of the site is?
