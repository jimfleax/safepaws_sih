# Playbook: Event

**UX priorities**: time-sensitive information (dates, locations, registration deadlines) must be
unambiguous and immediately findable — this is the one category where "creative but hard to find the
date" is a real, disqualifying failure regardless of visual craft.
**Visual priorities**: can support high creative/energetic expression (the event itself is often a
one-time or recurring cultural moment) as long as core logistics remain clear — see tension between
artistic expression and information clarity (`research/synthesis/12-design-tensions.md` tension #2).
**Information architecture**: date/location/registration should be reachable within the first screen or
two, regardless of how immersive the broader site is.
**Typical patterns**: countdown/schedule modules with real, accurate data (never a fake countdown, per
`principles/15-conversion.md`'s dark-pattern prohibition); registration flow follows strict form
convention (`patterns/forms.md`) even on an otherwise highly creative site.
**Appropriate motion**: energetic/anticipation-building motion appropriate for the hero/brand moment;
strict convention (minimal motion) for the actual registration/ticketing flow.
**Accessibility considerations**: venue accessibility information (if physical) should be genuinely
available, not an afterthought; digital accessibility baseline still fully applies regardless of how
experimental the creative treatment is.
**Responsive priorities**: assume many visitors will check event details on mobile while out — core
logistics must be fast and clear on a small screen even if desktop has richer creative treatment.
**Common mistakes**: burying the actual date/location/price behind creative navigation; fake urgency
countdowns; registration flows that break the convention-following discipline the moment matters most.
**Quality checklist**: Date/location/price found within seconds on any device? Registration flow follows
strict form convention regardless of the rest of the site's creative ambition? No fake urgency devices?
