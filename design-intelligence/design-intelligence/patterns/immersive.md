# Immersive Pattern
**What it is**: Full-viewport, often full-sensory (visual + sometimes audio) experiences that replace
conventional page structure — the highest-commitment, highest-risk pattern in this system.
**When to use**: A genuinely spatial/experiential concept exists in the underlying content or brand
(Iventions' events/spaces business, `research/awwwards/21-iventions.md`) and the audience specifically
values/rewards novelty over task efficiency (`principles/22-creative-web.md`).
**When NOT to use**: Any task-driven or transactional context — see tension #2 in
`research/synthesis/12-design-tensions.md`.
**Layout anatomy**: N/A in the conventional sense — layout is authored per-experience (3D scene
composition, spatial staging) rather than a reusable structural template.
**UX rationale**: Must still teach its own interaction model within the first few seconds (Bruno Simon's
physics-demonstration intro, `research/awwwards/07-bruno-simon.md` §1) — immersive does not mean
instruction-free.
**Visual variants**: Staged/spotlit 3D installation (Iventions); playable/game-world (Bruno Simon);
real-time input-responsive rendering (Active Theory, `research/awwwards/22-active-theory.md`).
**Responsive strategy**: Genuinely redesign for mobile/touch, or provide an explicit, honest simplified
alternative — do not assume a desktop-authored immersive experience "just works" reflowed.
**Accessibility requirements**: Provide a conventional/non-immersive alternative path to the same core
content wherever feasible — immersive formats have real, acknowledged accessibility limits
(`research/awwwards/07-bruno-simon.md` §11) and excluding users entirely is a significant cost to weigh.
**Performance considerations**: The highest performance risk category in this system — always ship a
lower-power fallback path, not just a "works on my machine" assumption.
**Anti-patterns**: Immersive treatment applied to content/products with no genuine spatial concept,
purely for spectacle.
**Implementation notes**: State explicitly, before building, what real concept the immersive format
serves — if that sentence can't be written, use a conventional pattern instead.
