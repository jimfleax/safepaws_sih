# Styles to Avoid

Structured record of aesthetic directions this user has confirmed they dislike — equally important as
`preferred-styles.md` for narrowing the design space efficiently.

## Format
```
- Direction: [name or description, e.g. "glassmorphism", "high-saturation purple/blue SaaS gradients"]
  Confirmed via: [specific project/statement, with date]
  Notes: [anything specific about the boundary — "dislikes decorative gradients but is fine with subtle
    atmosphere-only gradients like Stripe's"]
```

## Starting state
Empty — populated over time, same discipline as `preferred-styles.md`.

## The hard boundary (same as taste-profile.md)
A stated dislike never overrides an accessibility/usability/performance requirement in the other
direction either — e.g., disliking "excessive whitespace" doesn't license reducing spacing below what
`principles/12-accessibility.md`'s touch-target and readability requirements need. Aesthetic preference
operates entirely within the space the non-negotiable rules leave open.

## Relationship to anti-generic-ai
Many entries here will likely overlap with `anti-generic-ai/generic-ui-signatures.md`'s checklist
(gradient overuse, glassmorphism, card overuse) — that's expected and fine; a user's personal taste
converging with this system's general anti-generic guidance doesn't need to be treated as a
coincidence requiring resolution, just recorded as reinforcing evidence for that default.
