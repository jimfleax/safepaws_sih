# Creative Web / Experimental Design

## What it is
Portfolios, agency sites, and experiences whose primary goal is demonstrating craft/range to a
design-literate audience — the one category in this system where higher-risk, novelty-forward choices
(tension #2 in `research/synthesis/12-design-tensions.md`) are frequently the *correct* choice, not an
exception to avoid.

## Core pattern from this research
Bruno Simon's portfolio (`research/awwwards/07-bruno-simon.md`) is the clearest case study: its creator
explicitly, publicly named the format's usability tradeoffs and accepted them because the audience
(creative-industry peers, Awwwards jurors) specifically values novelty and technical demonstration over
task efficiency. Lusion's explicit rejection of a "safe" minimal default for their own studio site
(`research/awwwards/08-lusion.md` §1) is the same judgment from the agency side.

## Implementation guidance
- Before taking a high-novelty risk, explicitly confirm the audience is genuinely evaluation/craft-
  driven (peers, jurors, potential clients assessing capability) rather than task-driven — this is the
  resolving question for tension #2, and getting it wrong is the single biggest risk in this category.
- Every ambitious technical/visual device should map to a real concept or demonstrated capability (see
  `10-motion.md`, Iventions' spotlit-metaphor discipline) — spectacle disconnected from a stated idea
  reads as gimmick even to a sympathetic, craft-literate audience.
- Studio/portfolio sites can legitimately use bespoke, per-project layout systems (Resn, A24) rather than
  one enforced template, specifically to demonstrate range.

## Failure mode
Adopting this category's higher risk tolerance for a product that actually has a task-driven or
transactional audience — see tension #2's resolving question again; this is the most common
misapplication of "great creative-web design" lessons onto contexts that need them least.

## When to pull back toward convention even here
Even within creative-web contexts, primary conversion moments (a genuine "hire us" contact form) should
usually revert to convention-following form design (`17-form-design.md`) — demonstrated craft in the
experience shouldn't cost the actual business outcome at the one moment it matters most.
