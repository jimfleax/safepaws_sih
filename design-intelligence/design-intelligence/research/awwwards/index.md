## How to use this index

- Looking for a specific lesson (e.g. "how do I handle mobile nav for a content-heavy site")? Scan the
  Lesson column, then open the numbered dossier for full reasoning and the "when NOT to use" caveats.
- Looking for references for a specific brief? Use `retrieval/reference-index.json` (machine-readable,
  tag-based) rather than this table for programmatic lookup.
- Never cite a MODERATE-LOW or LOW confidence entry as if it were primary-source-confirmed — the
  confidence column exists specifically so that doesn't happen silently. See `RESEARCH_LOG.md` for the
  full accounting of why each tier was assigned.
