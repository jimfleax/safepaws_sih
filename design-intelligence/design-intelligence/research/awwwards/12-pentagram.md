## 13. Design Principles Extracted
1. **A multi-practitioner firm's portfolio can showcase range as a feature, not a liability**, if the
   site's IA makes each voice legible rather than blending them into an averaged house style. Use when a
   firm's actual differentiator is diverse expertise/perspective. Avoid forcing a single visual template
   onto genuinely different creative voices — it flattens the actual value proposition.
2. **A firm's organizational structure is itself a design decision that should inform the site's
   information architecture** — a partnership of equals needs a different IA than a single-founder
   studio; matching structure to IA is itself a form of honesty in the design.

## 14. Anti-Patterns
Do not force a single templated "case study" format onto genuinely different creative disciplines
(identity, architecture, product, editorial) if a firm's actual practice spans that range — doing so
would misrepresent the breadth that's the firm's real differentiator.
