## 13. Design Principles Extracted
1. **Game-engine-style real-time rendering (render loops, physics, dynamic camera systems) is a
   different technical and design approach from timeline-based scroll animation, with different
   strengths** — better suited to genuinely interactive, responsive-to-input experiences (games, product
   configurators, real-time simulations) than to narrative, linear scroll-driven storytelling, where
   timeline-based tools are usually a better fit. Use game-engine techniques specifically when the
   content needs to respond dynamically to unpredictable user input, not just to scroll position.
2. **A studio's specific historical role (pioneering a now-common technique before it was standard) is
   independently checkable evidence of genuine capability**, distinguishable from a studio that adopted
   an already-established technique well. When evaluating a reference studio's authority on a technique,
   check whether independent, competing sources credit them as an early adopter/pioneer specifically.

## 14. Anti-Patterns
Do not adopt game-engine-style real-time rendering techniques for content that is fundamentally linear
and narrative (most marketing/storytelling sites) — this is a mismatch of technique to content type;
the approach's real strength is dynamic responsiveness to unpredictable input, which most marketing
content does not need.

## 15. Transferable Patterns
- Real-time, input-responsive rendering (as distinct from pre-authored, timeline-based scroll animation)
  for genuinely interactive/simulation-like content.
