## 13. Design Principles Extracted
1. **"No-brand branding" — deliberately minimizing logo/decorative presence so the product itself
   carries the entire quality signal** — is a coherent, independently well-documented strategy distinct
   from generic minimalism; it works specifically because the underlying products are designed to
   justify that trust without ornamental support. Use when a brand's genuine differentiator is
   functional/material quality. Avoid for brands whose actual differentiator is aspirational identity/
   status signaling — there, deliberately downplaying brand presence works against the value being sold.
2. **Restraint applied consistently across an enormous, diverse product catalog (not just a curated hero
   selection) is a harder and more meaningful test of a minimal design system than restraint applied to
   a small portfolio** — worth noting explicitly when evaluating whether a minimal design system will
   actually scale.

## 14. Anti-Patterns
Do not adopt "no-brand" minimal presentation as a generic aesthetic shortcut disconnected from actual
product quality that would justify visitors' trust without brand reassurance — this pattern specifically
depends on the underlying product substance backing up the restraint.

## 15. Transferable Patterns
- "No-brand branding" (minimized logo/decorative presence, product quality carrying the trust signal).
- Restraint tested and maintained at large product-catalog scale, not just in a small curated portfolio.

## 19. Lessons for AI-Generated UI
Before defaulting to heavy branding (logo repetition, decorative flourish, badges) as a way to build
trust, an AI agent should consider whether — as this brand's well-documented philosophy argues — genuine
product/content substance, presented plainly, can carry the trust signal instead; and should test any
proposed minimal design system against a large, realistic content/product volume, not just a small
curated demo set, since restraint that only works for three hero items is not the same achievement as
restraint that holds across thousands.
