# Where References Genuinely Disagree

Not every tension resolves. These are places where two well-evidenced references point in different
directions — useful precisely because a system that hides disagreement teaches false universal rules.
See `research/synthesis/12-design-tensions.md` for the framework this feeds into.

## Motion volume: Lusion/Resn/Zajno (rich) vs. By-Kin/Are.na (restrained)
Both sides are independently well-evidenced. The resolving variable is not "which is better" but
"does this brief's differentiation depend on demonstrating technical/creative range (richness) or on
supporting sustained reading/trust (restraint)." Neither camp is wrong; they're answering different
briefs.

## Navigation depth: Framer (broad, role-based) vs. Obys (two items)
Framer serves a genuinely heterogeneous audience across many use cases; Obys serves a small number of
qualified clients by design. Copying Framer's nav breadth onto an Obys-style boutique business adds
noise; copying Obys's sparseness onto a broad-audience product hides things people need.

## Brand color: Cowboy/Lusion (near-zero color, achromatic chrome) vs. Zajno/Iventions (disciplined
saturated accents)
Both are disciplined, not sloppy — the difference is whether the *content* (product photography,
real-time 3D) or the *chrome* (UI accents) is meant to carry color. Achromatic chrome only works when
something else in the composition is already colorful; applied to genuinely monochrome content it
produces a flat result.

## Award-body signal weight: this system trusts Awwwards Developer Award independently of the Design
score (Iventions 21, Bruno Simon 07), but does not trust Design/SOTD score as a proxy for accessibility
or business outcome
This is a deliberate, evidenced position, not an oversight: Awwwards' own published Developer Award
criteria explicitly include mobile optimization and accessibility consideration, which the general
SOTD score does not isolate. Uncommon Studio (04) is the direct evidence for why SOTD score alone
cannot be trusted as an accessibility signal.

## Bespoke-per-project vs. systemized-template studios
Resn (06) and A24 (16) deliberately avoid a repeatable template, building each major project as its
own thing. Zajno (09) and Obys (05) show more evidence of a repeatable studio-wide standard (motion
architecture, type/grid discipline) applied project to project. Both produce award-level work. The
resolving variable: does the studio's/brand's own differentiation depend on demonstrated range
(bespoke) or on a recognizable consistent quality bar (systemized)?
