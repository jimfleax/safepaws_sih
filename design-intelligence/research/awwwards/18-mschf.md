## 13. Design Principles Extracted
1. **Build genuinely functional utility into a provocative concept, rather than a fake prop that only
   simulates function** — the credibility and shareability of the work depends on the function being
   real. Use when a concept's entire cultural relevance is that it's not just a stunt. Failure mode:
   announcing real functionality that turns out to be fake undermines trust catastrophically for this
   specific kind of brand.
2. **Deadpan seriousness applied to an absurd or provocative premise is a distinct rhetorical device
   from either straightforward marketing or pure comedy** — worth naming as its own category when
   evaluating unconventional brand work, since it succeeds or fails on a different axis (does the
   deadpan commitment hold, not "is it visually polished").

## 14. Anti-Patterns
Do not adopt deliberate provocation as a brand device without the organizational risk tolerance and
legal awareness this pattern genuinely requires — independently confirmed real legal action followed
at least one of this collective's projects; provocation-as-strategy has real, non-hypothetical
consequences.
